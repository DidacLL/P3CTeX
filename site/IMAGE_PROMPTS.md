## 1. Aesthetic Foundations & Visual Identity

All imagery across the P3CTeX documentation, showcases, and generator tool embodies the underground energy of indie hacker software, vintage computer engineering, and retrofuturistic academic craftsmanship:

- **Color Palette Tokens**:
  - **Terminal Phosphor Cyan**: `#73edff` (`rgb(115, 237, 255)`)
  - **CRT Amber Glow**: `#ffb454` (`rgb(255, 180, 84)`)
  - **Matrix Terminal Green**: `#38ef7d` (`rgb(56, 239, 125)`)
  - **Warez Crimson Accent**: `#ff5555`
  - **UOC Deep Academic Indigo**: `#000078`
  - **Deep CRT Canvas**: `#0a0f14` / `#0f1720`
  - **Weathered Parchment**: `#eee5d0` (`--sheet`)
- **Key Visual Elements**:
  - CRT monitor curvature, subtle scanlines, green/amber monochrome terminals, and chromatic aberration.
  - Box-drawing characters (`|`, `/`, `_`), ASCII/ANSI art typography, and segmented block letters.
  - Stacked compiler textbooks (Dragon book), soldering irons, breadboards, oscilloscopes, and coffee mugs on late-night study desks.
  - Dystopian cityscape visible through workshop windows, mechanical blueprint overlays, and isometric drafting grids.
- **Tone**:
  - Rebellious, intellectually intense, highly technical, encouraging, and authentic to the motto: *"Procrastinar és l'única esperança"*.
